Six safety basics
  1. Never type a password, a card number or an ID into any AI chat.
  2. Do not open or install files that strangers send you, and ignore anyone offering a "free upgrade" or a "hack" for an AI app.
  3. A real person's face or voice needs their yes before you put it in a picture, a video or a song.
  4. Before you sell something an AI tool made, read that tool's rules on selling. They differ.
  5. An AI can be confidently wrong. Ask it "is that a guess?", and check anything that matters somewhere else.
  6. Whatever you type into an AI app is sent to the company that runs it. Made-up characters and stories are fine. Leave out private details about real people, yourself included.
