The machine side

Small tools that check AI work.

passage ~ public
$ field-report-2026
An independent user's field report on Claude Code, the Claude desktop app and Claude in Chrome: 60 items, each with its severity, first-seen date and where it was reported, plus one page on how they were found and checked.
$ session-counter
Recounts failed requests, give-ups, watchdog kills and lost agents from your own Claude Code logs. Standard library only.
$ Glamor v0.8 is in testing.
$ more on the bench: blind frame checks for AI video, cut and loudness checks, quote and clock checks for agent replies. Being prepared for release.

The law of the house

  1. IEvery claim gets a source.Our field report says where its 60 items came from and how they were counted.
  2. IIEvery result gets checked.Its method page says how the items were checked against the records, and where the checking stops.
  3. IIIThe human calls it.Devon Fisher, the founder, makes the final call.

These are the rules our tools enforce. You can run them on your own AI with the free kit.
